# Interview question bank

Pick the questions that fit the gaps. Do not ask all of them.

## Research question
- In one sentence: what question does the thesis answer?
- What would count as an answer? What would surprise you?
- Are there sub-questions or hypotheses?
- Why does this question matter, and for whom? What is the expected contribution (empirical, methodological, theoretical)?
- What is explicitly out of scope?

## Dataset / corpus
- What exactly is the corpus (texts, period, language, genre, size)?
- Where does it come from? How do you access it, and under what licence or permissions?
- How was it, or will it be, selected? What is left out, and what biases follow from that?
- What preprocessing is planned (OCR quality, normalisation, annotation)?

## Method
- Which method(s) will you use, and with which tools?
- Why this method rather than an obvious alternative?
- How will you evaluate or validate the results (close reading, ground truth, inter-annotator agreement, …)?
- How do the computational parts and the interpretive parts relate?

## Field
- Which discipline(s) and subfield(s) do you position yourself in?
- Which debates or schools are relevant? Who are the key authors, and who are their critics?
- Which works are closest to yours, and how will yours differ?

## Structure
- What is the planned chapter outline?
- Roughly how long should each part be (share of the total, or characters)?

## Constraints
- Deadline and milestones?
- Length limit (characters/words/pages)? Citation and style guide?
- What do your advisors expect or emphasise? Is there an official grading rubric?
