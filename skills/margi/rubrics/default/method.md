# Methodological transparency

Could a peer understand, assess and, where applicable, reproduce what was done? Judge this
against `margi/docs/method.md` and `dataset.md`.

- **5**: Method, data, parameters, tools and limitations are explicit and justified against alternatives.
- **3**: Method is described, but choices are not justified, or the description lacks details needed to assess it.
- **1**: Method unclear, or mismatched with what the docs say is planned.

Look for: undocumented preprocessing, unexplained parameter choices, missing validation or
evaluation, no reflection on corpus bias or the limits of operationalisation.
