# Engagement with scholarship

Does the section position itself within the field described in `margi/docs/field.md`?

- **5**: Engages critically with the relevant debates, including counterpositions, and makes its own position clear.
- **3**: Cites relevant work, but mostly summarises it, or misses an obvious camp or debate.
- **1**: Little or no engagement, outdated or tangential sources only.

Look for: listing instead of synthesis ("X says…, Y says…"), missing canonical works or
critics named in `field.md`, no statement of how this thesis differs.
