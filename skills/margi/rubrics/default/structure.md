# Structure and coherence

Is the section organised so that a reader can follow it?

- **5**: Clear internal structure, signposting, smooth transitions, appropriate length (see `structure.md` targets).
- **3**: Followable, but some paragraphs are misplaced, repetitive or lack transitions.
- **1**: Disorganised. The reader cannot reconstruct the line of thought.

Look for: paragraphs with several topics, repeated content, sections much longer or shorter
than planned, conclusions that introduce new material.
