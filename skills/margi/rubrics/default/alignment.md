# Alignment with research question

Does this section serve the research question and contribution documented in
`margi/docs/research-question.md`?

- **5**: Every part visibly advances the RQ, and the section's role in the thesis is explicit.
- **3**: Mostly relevant. Some passages drift, or their link to the RQ is left implicit.
- **1**: Unclear or no connection to the RQ, or it pursues a different question.

Look for: scope drift, passages that would fit a different thesis, sub-questions promised but
not addressed, terms used differently than in the docs.
