# Evidence and sources

Are claims backed by appropriate evidence: primary sources, data, results or citations?

- **5**: Claims are consistently supported. Evidence is apt, correctly cited and interpreted with care.
- **3**: Mostly supported. Some claims lack sources, or evidence is over-read.
- **1**: Key claims are unsupported, or the evidence is misrepresented.

Look for: uncited factual claims, quotations without page numbers, results reported without
the data or parameters needed to interpret them, examples cherry-picked from a corpus.
