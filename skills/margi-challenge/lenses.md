# Challenge lenses

Use the lenses that fit the aspect, and phrase every challenge concretely for this thesis.

## Assumptions
What has to be true for the plan to work? Which of those is unexamined? What happens if it is false?

## Scope
Is the question answerable within the deadline, length and corpus documented in
`constraints.md` and `dataset.md`? Is it too broad ("literature in the 19th century") or too
narrow to matter?

## Operationalisation
How do abstract concepts (e.g. "canon", "style", "influence") become something measurable or
observable? Is the operationalisation defensible in the discipline? What does it miss?

## Validity
- Do the methods actually answer the question (construct validity)?
- Could results come from corpus artefacts, OCR noise, selection or parameter choices?
- How far can conclusions generalise beyond the corpus?

## Alternatives
What is the obvious alternative method, corpus or framing, and why is it not used? Would a
reviewer from the neighbouring camp (e.g. close reading vs. distant reading) accept the choice?

## Positioning and contribution
So what? What will the field know afterwards that it did not know before? Who has done
something close, and what exactly is different?

## Consistency
Do the research question, method, dataset and planned structure fit together? Does the
structure give the most space to what the RQ makes central?

## Ethics and data
Copyright, licensing, personal data, representation of marginalised voices in the corpus.
