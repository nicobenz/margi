# Proposal

<!--
Your thesis plan, in your own words. Write freely: fragments, bullet points, doubts and
half-finished thoughts are all fine. Nothing here needs to be polished.

When you have something under most headings, commit this file and run `/margi propose`
in your agent. margi turns it into structured project docs in margi/docs/ and lists what
is still unclear in margi/docs/open-questions.md. Add to this file and run it again as
often as you like. margi never edits this file.

The comments under each heading are prompts, not a form. Delete them whenever you like.
-->

## Title ideas
<!-- Working titles, even rough ones. -->

## Research question
<!-- The question in one sentence. Sub-questions or hypotheses? What would count as an
answer, and what would surprise you? What is out of scope? -->

## Motivation
<!-- Why does this question matter, and for whom? -->

## Field and related work
<!-- Which discipline and debates? Key authors and their critics? Which works are closest
to yours, and how does yours differ? -->

## Data / corpus
<!-- What exactly (texts, period, language, genre, size)? Where from, and under what
licence or access? How selected, and what is left out? Preprocessing? -->

## Method
<!-- What will you do, with which tools? Why this method and not an obvious alternative?
How will you check that the results hold? -->

## Expected contribution
<!-- Empirical, methodological, theoretical? What will be known afterwards that isn't now? -->

## Planned outline
<!-- Chapters and roughly how long each should be. -->

## Constraints
<!-- Deadline and milestones, length limit, style and citation guide, what your advisors
expect or emphasise, an official grading rubric. -->

## Open thoughts
<!-- Anything else: doubts, alternatives you're weighing, things you don't know yet. -->
